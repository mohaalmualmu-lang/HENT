Acute angle-closure glaucoma

Acute angle-closure glaucoma is an eye emergency: the drainage angle closes and pressure rises within hours. Patients report severe pain, blurred vision, halos around lights, nausea and vomiting.

  • Keep the patient supine (lying flat) and transport without delay.
  • Normal intraocular pressure is 10–21 mmHg; in an attack it is often ≥ 40 mmHg.
  • Pupil: mid-dilated → fixed (متوسع وثابت).
